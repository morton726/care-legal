Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

In short: C.A.R.E. SimBalloon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or patient information. Every vital sign, rhythm, and waveform in the app is synthetic — generated by the app's own simulation engine — and none of it ever leaves your device or local network.

1. What this app is

C.A.R.E. SimBalloon is a training simulator for intra-aortic balloon pump (IABP) therapy, built for nursing, perfusion, paramedic, and critical care education. It is not a medical device and is not intended for use in the treatment or monitoring of real patients. All patient data displayed in the app — vitals, ECG, arterial pressure, and balloon-pressure waveforms — is synthetic and generated locally by the app. The app is never connected to a patient or to any real medical equipment.

2. Information we collect

We collect none. C.A.R.E. SimBalloon has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising identifiers. The table below covers every category of data the app touches:

CategoryCollected?Detail
Patient / simulation dataNoAll vitals and waveforms are synthetic and generated on-device by the simulation engine; nothing is recorded or transmitted.
Personal identifiersNoNo name, email, or account is required to use the app.
LocationNoThe app does not access or request location data.
Usage analytics / trackingNoNo analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
Local network pairingDevice-local onlyThe console and instructor devices discover and connect to each other directly over your local Wi-Fi network. Session data (rhythm, scenario, timing and pump settings) is exchanged only between those two paired devices and never reaches our servers, because we don't have any.
Subscription & paymentHandled entirely by ApplePurchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We never see your payment details — Apple manages billing and simply confirms your subscription status to the app.

3. Local network permission

iOS will ask for local network permission the first time you pair two devices. This permission is used solely to discover and connect the console and instructor devices on the same Wi-Fi network via Bonjour and a direct TCP connection. This traffic stays on your local network and is never sent to the internet.

4. Children's privacy

C.A.R.E. SimBalloon is intended for clinical education audiences and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age, there is no data to disclose, sell, or remove.

5. Data retention

There is nothing to retain. Session logs and settings are held only in the app's memory during use and are not persisted to any server we control.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and post the revised version at this same address.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to CAREAPPSupport@csu-als.org.
